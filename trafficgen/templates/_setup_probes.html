Probe interfaces & recommend roles
Evidence from each data NIC, including interfaces without an IPv4 address.

Provide the data gateway IPs you know. The app sends a bounded ARP probe on each of up to six data NICs, reads available neighbor/LLDP evidence, and checks exposed vendor identity on a limited number of already-addressed connections. It does not guess subnets, assign IPs, install routes or switch your workload. Down links may be enabled briefly and restored after probing.
